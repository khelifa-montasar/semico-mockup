What changed from V3

  • MAX485 + 1k + 2k divider → MAX3485 (3.3V). No divider, no 5V. Same pins, same code.
  • IRLZ44N + 1N4007 + 10k → DRV8833 channel A (pump). Diodes and pull-downs are inside.
  • New: DRV8833 channel B switches the probe power. The probe is fully OFF between readings.
  • New: 470 µF capacitor in both modes. 3A fuse and battery level (G34) in battery mode.
  • Wired mode: the ESP32 VIN pin is no longer used.

Safety rules

  • Set each MT3608 with nothing connected. Measure with a multimeter. Glue the screw. Write "5V" or "12V" on it.
  • MAX3485 VCC = 3.3V only. 5V can damage it.
  • 470 µF capacitor: long leg = +. The wrong way can pop it.
  • Keep the 12V wires away from the ESP32 pins. One touch kills the ESP32.
  • Pump and power wires: short and tight. Best in a Wago or a screw terminal.
  • Battery mode: keep the Li-Po out of the sun and away from water.

Pin names on your modules

  • Some DRV8833 boards use other names: AIN1 = IN1, AIN2 = IN2, BIN1 = IN3, BIN2 = IN4, AO1/AO2 = OUT1/OUT2, BO1/BO2 = OUT3/OUT4.
  • DRV8833 VM may be labeled VCC. EEP may be labeled SLP or nSLEEP. Leave the ULT / FAULT pin empty.
  • Voltage sensor: the screw terminal (VCC, GND) is the input. Use only the S output pin.

Code notes

  • Pump: G25 HIGH = ON. Always set a max ON time in the code.
  • Probe: G26 HIGH → wait the warm-up time (see the probe datasheet) → read Modbus → G26 LOW.
  • Don't read the probe while the pump is starting (the voltage dips for a moment).
  • RS485: G4 HIGH while sending, LOW to listen (ModbusMaster: preTransmission / postTransmission).
  • Watchdog ON: if the ESP32 freezes, it restarts. At restart G25 goes LOW, so the pump stops.
  • Battery (G34): battery volts = pin volts × 5. Check once with a multimeter. Under 3.3V → no pump, deep sleep.

Why DRV8833 and not a relay module

  • Most cheap relay modules need 5V. With a 3.3V ESP32 pin, many do not switch OFF.
  • A relay coil uses about 70 mA while it is ON. That is bad for the battery.
  • Some relay modules switch ON for a moment at boot, so the pump starts by itself.
  • With a relay you still need a diode across the pump.
  • One DRV8833 does 2 jobs: pump + probe power. With relays you need 2 relays.
  • Use a relay later, for a big pump or a 220V pump (through a contactor).
